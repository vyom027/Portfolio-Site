CloudFusion / Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Overview

CloudFusion is a personal utility used to connect and mount a user's own Google Drive storage on a local computer. This privacy policy explains how CloudFusion handles information when Google Drive access is authorized.

2. Google Account Access

CloudFusion uses Google's OAuth 2.0 authorization to obtain permission to access the Google Drive account selected by the user. Access is granted by the user through Google's authorization screen.

3. Information We Collect

CloudFusion does not operate a separate backend service that collects or stores users' Google Drive files, passwords, or Google account credentials. The application is intended for local use.

4. How Google Drive Data Is Used

Google Drive access is used only to provide the functionality requested by the user, such as browsing, reading, writing, and mounting files through the local rclone configuration. CloudFusion does not sell Google Drive data or use it for advertising.

5. Storage of OAuth Credentials

OAuth configuration and refresh tokens used by rclone are stored as part of the user's local rclone configuration. They are not intentionally uploaded by CloudFusion to a separate CloudFusion server.

6. Sharing of Information

CloudFusion does not sell or share Google Drive content with third parties. Google services may process data according to Google's own policies when the user authorizes access and uses Google Drive.

7. User Control

The user can revoke CloudFusion's access to their Google account at any time through their Google Account security settings. The user can also remove the local rclone configuration from their computer.

8. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or CloudFusion, please contact the developer through the contact information available on the portfolio homepage.
